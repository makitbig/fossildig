Privacy Policy

Fossil Dig: Dino Museum · Effective November 8, 2026

한국어

The short version

  • We have no servers and no accounts. Your museum and progress stay on your device.
  • Ads in the free version are served by Google AdMob, which may use device identifiers.
  • We do not run our own analytics or collect gameplay data.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

1. Data stored on your device

Your collected fossils, museum, coins, seasons, settings and language are stored only in the app's storage on your device. We cannot see them, and they are deleted when you delete the app.

2. Analytics and crash reports

The app does not send analytics or crash reports to us. If we ever add them, we will update this policy before the version that includes them is released.

3. Advertising

The free version shows a small banner on the dig screen and, only when you choose to watch one, rewarded videos from Google AdMob. There are no ads between digs. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, advertising ID on Android), if you allow it;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped;
  • diagnostic data, such as ad loading errors and performance.

Your consent. On iOS the app asks whether to allow tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not based on your activity in other companies' apps and websites. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choices before ads are shown; you can change them at any time from Menu › Ad privacy. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.

Buying Pro removes the banner and lets you collect rewards without watching ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Purchases

Fossil Dig Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. The app checks on your device whether Pro has been purchased; we have no server and never receive your name, Apple or Google account, payment card or billing details. Coins cannot be bought with money. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

5. Email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply, and delete them when the conversation is over.

6. Children

Fossil Dig is a general-audience game that families can enjoy together. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has contacted us, email us and we will delete the information.

7. Your choices

  • Turn tracking off at any time in your device's privacy settings (iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking).
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, review your ad choices from Menu › Ad privacy.
  • Delete all game data by deleting the app.
  • Ask us any question about your data at the address below.

8. Changes and contact

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
